§ 02 / The protocol

A loan lifecycle, in five
immutable steps.

Step 01 · Genesis

The contract is born.

Borrower deploys a master contract. Parameters locked forever: amount, term, interest rate, tranche structure. No party can amend them after deployment.

Step 02 · Skin in the game

Tranche C funded.

Borrower funds Tranche C (10%) with their own capital. They lose it first in a default. Skin in the game, enforced in code — not in covenants.

Step 03 · Funding

Investors fund the stack.

Retail investors buy Senior tokens (12% p.a.). Risk investors buy Mezzanine (18% p.a.). Contract transitions: COLLECTING → ACTIVE.

Step 04 · Maturity

Maturity. No phone calls.

At maturity, the backend monitors on-chain state. No human action needed. Funds are distributed in roughly 15 seconds.

Step 05 · Waterfall

Senior first. Always.

Capital flows in priority order: Senior → Mezzanine → Junior. The borrower receives last. The waterfall is immutable — written into bytecode, not into a clause.
